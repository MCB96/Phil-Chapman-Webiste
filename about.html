ABOUT THE BUSINESS

Experience with aerials,
satellite and TV set-up.

Phil Chapman Aerial Services is a family-run company established in 1961. The existing business website states over 25 years of experience in aerial installations and local knowledge in Nottingham.

The work includes installing and repairing TV aerials and satellite dishes, helping with reception problems, fitting FM and DAB aerials and setting up televisions and related equipment.

The business describes its approach as making sure customers are happy with the quality of the work and understand how their equipment works.
