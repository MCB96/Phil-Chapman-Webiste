ABOUT THE BUSINESS

Good, practical help
for your home.

Phil Chapman Aerial Services is a family-run company established in 1961. The business provides aerial and satellite installations, repairs, reception troubleshooting, radio aerials and television set-up in Nottingham and surrounding areas.

Phil’s published approach is to explain how the equipment works and to make sure customers understand the work carried out. If something has gone wrong with your signal, or you are planning a new installation, you can call for free advice and a quote.

I won't leave a job until you are happy with the quality of the work undertaken and have a good understanding of how everything works.
